Our Services

What We Offer

Providing regulated, high-quality spaces equipped to handle events of any scale, driven by excellence and transparency in government service.

Facility Reservations

Access our three top-tier venues: the Provincial Gymnasium, Gov. Ben Palispis Auditorium, and the PCL Hall. Ideal for everything from sprawling sports events to corporate seminars.

Equipment Rentals

Need more chairs, tables, or a specialized sound system? We offer comprehensive equipment rentals directly from the provincial office, added seamlessly to your reservation.

Transparent & Secure

Our pricing is completely transparent, backed by official tax ordinances. Your payments and security deposits are handled efficiently from quotation to refund.
